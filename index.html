Questions

Answered.

When is Sanctum launching?+

Sanctum at Mahindra Citadel is in its launch phase with the EOI window already open. Pre-registered buyers receive priority allocation and launch-phase pricing on Towers E, F & G.

Where is the project located?+

CTS No. 4854, Sub-Plot A, Old Mumbai-Pune Highway, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune – 411018 — directly opposite Sant Tukaram Metro Station, with Pimpri Metro 1.6 km away.

What configurations are available?+

Premium 2 & 3 BHK residences from 743 sqft to 1377 sqft, with select larger 3 & 4 BHK homes. All with terrace-style decks, cross-ventilation, and master bedroom wardrobe niches.

What amenities does the project offer?+

49,900 sqft across two clubhouses — half Olympic-size pool, badminton courts, indoor community hall, gym, cinema lounge, dedicated pet park, kids' play areas, and ~7 acres of open-to-sky spaces.

Who is the developer?+

Mahindra Lifespace Developers Ltd — 31+ years of legacy, India's first Net Zero project, and a 100% green portfolio since 2014. Mahindra Citadel is their 7th Pimpri landmark.

Is it a good investment?+

Comparable Mahindra projects in Pimpri (Antheia, Centralis) deliver 6-7% rental yields versus the 4-5% Pimpri average. Metro adjacency, IGBC Gold certification, and upcoming infrastructure drive sustained appreciation.

How do I book a site visit?+

Site visits are by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 88570 90799 and our team will arrange a private visit at your preferred time.
